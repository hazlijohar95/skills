# Position memo

One memo per judgment: a stream policy, a contract, or a modification. It is written for a
reviewer who has the documents and not the conversation. It passes when that reviewer can
trace every conclusion to a document without asking anyone.

```markdown
# Revenue position: <customer or stream>, <contract id>

**Prepared:** <date> · **Period affected:** <first period> · **Status:** <draft / approved by <who> on <date>>

## Question
<the accounting question in one or two sentences, in terms of this contract>

## Facts
<numbered, each quoting or pointing to its document: "Order form HB-2025-201: 'neither
party may terminate for convenience'">

## Terms checked
<every term on reference/reading-list.md: the ones present, each with its file, then
"absent: all others". This is the record that the list was walked.>

## Analysis
<one subsection per question of the five that needed judgment. Each gives the conclusion,
the deciding fact by number, the rejected treatment and the fact that rejects it, and the
principle applied. Questions settled by the stream policy get one line citing it.>

## Conclusion
<the treatment, in two to four sentences>

## Numbers
<the closekit commands run and their output. Period revenue, balances, and the difference
from what was booked, by account>

## Elections and estimates relied on
<each election and each estimate, with its source and the date it is next reassessed>

## Challenge
<the challenger's strongest objection and how it was resolved, or "not run" with the
second-review setting that excluded it>

## References
<rows from reference/codification.md only>
```

Rules:
- Facts are quoted, not paraphrased, when the wording decides the answer.
- A rejected alternative is argued properly, not set up to fail. A reviewer should see why
  a reasonable preparer might have chosen it.
- No figure is typed that the Numbers section's kit output does not show.
