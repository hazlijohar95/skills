# Who controls before transfer

Apply when a third party sits between the entity and the end user: a marketplace, a
reseller, a referral partner, a subcontractor, a payment platform.

First identify the customer: the party that contracted for the entity's goods or services
in exchange for consideration. Then for each specified good or service, ask whether the
entity controls it before it reaches the customer. Controlling it makes the entity a
principal, recognizing the gross amount. Arranging for someone else to provide it makes
the entity an agent, recognizing its fee or commission.

**Why.** Gross versus net can double or halve reported revenue with no change in profit.
Readers compare revenue across companies. A marketplace that collects and keeps a fee does
not turn a software company into a 97%-revenue business. A reseller that sets its own
price does not make its markup the software company's revenue.

**Pattern.**
- Marketplace: whose terms govern the end user, who sets the price, and does the
  marketplace have any obligation to provide the product? The seller's EULA, the seller's
  price, and no fulfillment obligation mean the end user is the customer. The seller is
  principal and books gross. The marketplace fee is a selling cost, usually a cost of
  obtaining the contract (see costs-follow-the-revenue).
- Reseller: does the reseller buy and then sell on its own terms and price, carrying the
  credit risk? Then the reseller is the customer, and revenue is the price to the
  reseller. What the reseller charges its customer is not the entity's revenue.
- Subcontracted services the entity integrates into its own deliverable, where it is
  responsible for acceptability: principal. A referral where the other party contracts
  and delivers: agent.
- The indicators (primary responsibility, inventory risk, price discretion) support the
  control conclusion. They don't replace it.

**Deciding facts.** Which agreement the end user signs. Who sets the end price. Who is
paid by whom, and whether payment depends on the end user paying. Who fixes problems.

**The tell.** Revenue equal to cash remitted by a marketplace. Revenue grossed up to a
reseller's price.

**Codification.** 606-10-55-36, 55-36A, 55-39.
