# Enforceable rights set the contract

Apply when reading any contract: term, termination, side letters, related contracts,
collectibility.

The contract for accounting purposes is what each side can enforce, not what the order
form's title page says. Its term ends where the enforceable rights end. Its terms include
every document the parties treat as binding.

**Why.** A three-year order form the customer can walk away from on 60 days' notice
without penalty obliges the customer to pay for 60 days. Accounting for three years books
revenue the entity has no right to collect. The opposite error hides in side letters:
an email granting free months or a cancellation right changes the deal the order form
describes.

**Pattern.**
- Find every termination right. For each: who holds it, the notice period, and what it
  costs to use. A penalty that is substantive (economically meaningful against the
  benefits of continuing) keeps the longer term. A trivial or absent one shortens the term
  to the notice period.
- Read the special terms section, amendments, and emails from sales before the MSA. The
  deviation is where the answer changes.
- Contracts signed with the same customer at or near the same time get combined when they
  were negotiated together, when one's price depends on the other, or when together they
  form one promise. A $1 order form next to a heavily discounted services SOW is one
  contract.
- Collectibility is judged on the customer's ability and intent to pay the amount the
  entity expects to be entitled to, which may be less than the list price when a
  concession is likely. Failing it means no contract yet. Cash received sits as a
  liability.
- Cash collected for periods beyond the enforceable term, refundable if the customer
  exits, is a refund liability, not deferred revenue. Carve it out in the contract file
  (`refund_liability`) so the kit presents it apart.
- Month-to-month after an initial term is a new one-month contract each month, unless a
  renewal price creates a material right (see options-can-be-promises).

**Deciding facts.** Termination clause and its penalty. Special terms that supersede the
MSA. Side letters and sales emails. Other order forms with the same customer within a few
weeks. Credit history.

**The tell.** A multi-year schedule, a multi-year ramp averaged, or a multi-year remaining
performance obligation figure for a contract with a free exit.

**Codification.** 606-10-25-1, 25-2, 25-3, 25-7, 25-9. Termination guidance comes from TRG
discussion (nonauthoritative).
