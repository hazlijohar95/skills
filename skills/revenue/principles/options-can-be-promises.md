# Options can be promises

Apply when a contract offers renewals, add-ons, or future purchases at a set price, charges
a one-time fee up front, or sells credits or prepaid rights the customer may not use.

An option to buy more later is a performance obligation when it gives the customer a
discount it would not get without this contract. Part of today's price then pays for that
future discount, and that part is recognized when the option is used or expires. Rights
the customer pays for and never uses (breakage) are recognized in proportion to the rights
used, when the entity can expect breakage.

**Why.** A customer who pays $30,000 in year one and $24,000 at every renewal paid part of
the renewals up front. Booking the whole $30,000 in year one front-loads revenue that the
renewals earn. Prepaid credits are the mirror image. Waiting until expiry to recognize
unused credits back-loads revenue the entity already expects to keep.

**Pattern.**
- Compare the option price with what a customer without this contract would pay. A
  discount beyond the normal range available to that class of customer is a material
  right. Pricing at standalone selling price is not.
- Upfront fees for setup work: the fee is an advance payment. Recognize it over the
  subscription. When renewals skip the fee, ask whether that is a material right. If it
  is, the fee runs over the expected customer life, or is allocated using the renewal
  alternative (similar services on the original terms). If it isn't, the fee runs over the
  initial term. Show the comparison either way.
- Prepaid credits: estimate breakage from portfolio history when it is predictive. Spread
  the price over expected usage, not purchased units. Without predictive history,
  recognize unused credits only when use becomes remote. Check unclaimed property rules
  before treating breakage as revenue.
- A discount on future purchases handed out to win this deal (a coupon, an MFN clause) is
  an option to test the same way.

**Deciding facts.** Renewal and add-on pricing against new-customer pricing. Customer
life data. Breakage history and whether it is stable. Refund and extension practice.

**The tell.** Implementation fee revenue at go-live. Credits recognized at list
price per unit with the rest at expiry.

**Codification.** 606-10-55-41 to 55-43, 55-45, 55-46 to 55-49, 55-50 to 55-53.
