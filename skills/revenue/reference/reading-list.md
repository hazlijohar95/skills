# Contract terms that change the answer

Read every contract against this list after working the five questions, and whenever
triaging a contract against a stream policy. Each term present is a deviation to reason
through with the principle named. The list finds terms. It never decides them.

| Term to look for | Where it hides | Why it matters | Principle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Termination for convenience, and its penalty | Special terms, MSA termination section | Shortens the contract to the notice period | enforceable-rights |
| Non-cancellable multi-year with rising or falling prices | Order form pricing table | Fixed total spread over identical periods | price-is-what-you-expect, enforceable-rights |
| Free months, ramps, first-year discounts | Order form, deal-desk notes | Same as above; also financing if payments lag | price-is-what-you-expect |
| Side letters, sales emails granting terms | Email, CRM notes | Part of the contract if the parties treat them as binding | enforceable-rights |
| Other orders with the same customer near the same date | Billing system | Combination | enforceable-rights |
| One-time setup, onboarding, or implementation fee | Order form | Activity or separate promise; possible material right | find-what-the-customer-is-buying, options |
| Professional services next to a subscription | SOW | Distinct or not; SSP allocation | find-what-the-customer-is-buying, allocate |
| On-premise or self-hosting rights | MSA license grant, exit terms | A license inside a SaaS deal | find-what-the-customer-is-buying |
| Usage, overage, tiers, minimum commitments | Order form, pricing exhibit | Variable consideration, often allocated to one period | price, allocate |
| SLA credits, service credits, penalties | SLA addendum | Variable consideration reducing revenue | price |
| Retrospective rebates, volume tiers applied to past purchases | Engagement letter, pricing exhibit | Invoices no longer track value | price, pattern |
| Success fees, bonuses, holdbacks, acceptance | SOW | Variable consideration and the constraint | price |
| Caps, not-to-exceed amounts | SOW | Limits the price; can stop right-to-invoice | price, pattern |
| Fixed-fee services | SOW | Measure of progress, estimates at completion | pattern |
| Renewal price below new-customer price, discount on future purchases | Renewal clause, order form | Material right | options |
| Prepaid credits, tokens, hours that expire | Order form | Breakage | options |
| Refund rights, satisfaction guarantees | MSA, order form | Refund liability, variable consideration | price, balances |
| Payment far from delivery (over a year) | Billing terms | Financing component | price |
| Credits, rebates, or payments to the customer; marketing funds | Order form, side letters | Consideration payable to a customer | price |
| Marketplace or reseller in the chain | Channel agreements | Who the customer is; gross or net | who-controls |
| Amendments, add-ons, downgrades, early renewals, concessions | Amendments, deal-desk approvals | Modification method | modifications |
| Commission plan terms | Sales compensation plan | Capitalization and amortization period | costs |
| Collectibility doubts: new customer in distress, history of concessions | Credit notes, AR aging | Whether a contract exists; implicit price concessions | enforceable-rights, price |
