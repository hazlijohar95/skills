# Codification references

Cite from this table only. Each row was checked against the named public guide in
September 2026. A topic missing here is cited by name ("the series guidance"), never by
a paragraph number from memory. When the FASB amends a paragraph, update this table first.

Sources: Deloitte DART Roadmap: Revenue Recognition (DART), PwC Viewpoint Revenue from
contracts with customers guide (PwC), KPMG Handbook: Software and SaaS revenue (KPMG),
RevenueHub (RH).

| Topic | ASC | Rule | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contract exists | 606-10-25-1 | Five criteria: approved and committed, rights identified, payment terms identified, commercial substance, collection probable. | RH, DART |
| No contract yet | 606-10-25-7 | Consideration received is a liability until no obligations remain and it is nonrefundable, or the contract is terminated and it is nonrefundable. | RH |
| Contract term | 606-10-25-2, 25-3 | The contract lasts as long as the parties have present enforceable rights and obligations. A termination-for-convenience right with no substantive penalty shortens the term to the notice period. | PwC, RH (TRG discussions, not codified) |
| Combining contracts | 606-10-25-9 | Contracts with the same customer at or near the same time are combined when negotiated as one package, when one's price depends on the other, or when together they form one performance obligation. | RH |
| Modification defined | 606-10-25-10, 25-11 | A change in scope or price the parties approve. | DART |
| Modification as separate contract | 606-10-25-12 | Added distinct goods or services, priced at standalone selling price adjusted for the contract's circumstances. | DART |
| Modification otherwise | 606-10-25-13 | (a) Remaining goods or services distinct: terminate the old contract and start a new one, prospectively. (b) Not distinct, part of a partly satisfied obligation: cumulative catch-up. (c) A mix: apply each to its part. | DART |
| Series | 606-10-25-14(b), 25-15 | Distinct goods or services that are substantially the same with the same pattern of transfer are one performance obligation. Stand-ready SaaS usually qualifies. | DART, RH |
| Distinct | 606-10-25-19 to 25-21 | Capable of being distinct and distinct in the context of the contract. 25-21 gives three indicators that it is not separately identifiable: significant integration, significant modification or customization, high interdependence. | PwC, RH |
| Over time | 606-10-25-27 | Any one of: customer receives and consumes as performed; the work creates or enhances an asset the customer controls; no alternative use plus an enforceable right to payment for performance to date. | PwC |
| Variable consideration | 606-10-32-5 to 32-9 | Credits, rebates, bonuses, penalties, usage and similar items are estimated by expected value or most likely amount, whichever predicts better. | DART, PwC |
| Constraint | 606-10-32-11 to 32-13 | Include variable consideration only to the extent a significant reversal is probable not to occur. | DART, PwC |
| Reassess | 606-10-32-14 | Update the estimate at each reporting date. | DART |
| Financing | 606-10-32-15 to 32-17 | Adjust for a significant financing component. 32-17 lists when none exists, including a difference that arises for reasons other than financing. | DART |
| Financing expedient | 606-10-32-18 | No adjustment when payment and transfer are expected to be within one year of each other. | DART |
| Consideration payable to a customer | 606-10-32-25 to 32-27 | Reduces the transaction price unless it pays for a distinct good or service from the customer. Recognized at the later of the related revenue and the payment or promise to pay. | DART |
| SSP estimation | 606-10-32-33, 32-34 | Maximize observable inputs. Adjusted market assessment, expected cost plus margin, or residual. Residual only when SSP is highly variable or uncertain. | PwC, DART |
| Discount allocation | 606-10-32-36, 32-37 | A discount goes to all obligations proportionally, unless observable evidence shows it belongs entirely to a subset. | PwC, DART |
| Variable consideration allocation | 606-10-32-39 to 32-41 | Allocate a variable amount entirely to one obligation, or to one distinct period in a series, when its terms relate specifically to that part and the result meets the allocation objective. | DART, PwC |
| Presentation | 606-10-45-1 to 45-4 | Contract liability when payment precedes performance. Contract asset when the right is conditional on something other than time. Receivable when unconditional. Net asset and liability within one contract (ASU 2014-09 basis for conclusions BC317). | DART, RH |
| Principal or agent | 606-10-55-36, 55-36A, 55-39 | Principal if it controls the specified good or service before transfer. The indicators in 55-39 (fulfillment responsibility, inventory risk, price discretion) support the control test and do not replace it. | DART |
| Progress | 606-10-55-16 to 55-21 | Output methods (55-17) or input methods (55-20). Exclude inputs that do not depict transfer, such as wasted effort. | PwC, DART |
| Right to invoice | 606-10-55-18 | Recognize the amount invoiceable when it corresponds directly to the value of performance to date. | PwC, DART |
| Material right | 606-10-55-41 to 55-43 | An option is a performance obligation only if it gives a discount incremental to what the customer would get without the contract. | PwC |
| Renewal alternative | 606-10-55-45 | For renewals of similar goods or services on the original terms, allocate by reference to the expected renewals and their consideration. | PwC, DART |
| Breakage | 606-10-55-46 to 55-49 | Expected breakage recognized in proportion to rights exercised. Otherwise when exercise becomes remote. Amounts owed under unclaimed property laws stay liabilities. | DART, PwC |
| Upfront fees | 606-10-55-50 to 55-53 | A nonrefundable upfront fee usually pays in advance for future services, recognized as they are provided, over a longer period when a renewal option gives a material right. Setup activities are not a service. | DART, PwC |
| Hosting | 606-10-55-54 | A hosting arrangement contains a software license only if the customer can take possession without significant penalty and run it themselves or through another host (criteria in 985-20-15-5). | DART, KPMG |
| License nature | 606-10-55-58 to 55-62 | Functional IP (right to use) transfers at a point in time. Symbolic IP (right to access) transfers over time. | DART |
| Usage-based royalty | 606-10-55-65, 55-65A | Applies only to licenses of IP, and only when the license is predominant. SaaS usage fees fall under general variable consideration. | DART |
| Portfolio | 606-10-10-4 | Apply to a portfolio of similar contracts when the result would not differ materially from contract-by-contract. | DART, PwC |
| Nonpublic disclosure | 606-10-50-7, 50-16 | Nonpublic entities may limit disaggregation to timing of transfer and may skip the remaining performance obligations disclosure. | DART |
| Contract costs: obtaining | 340-40-25-1, 25-2 | Capitalize incremental costs of obtaining a contract the entity expects to recover. | RH |
| Contract costs: expedient | 340-40-25-4 | May expense when the amortization period would be one year or less. | RH |
| Contract costs: fulfilling | 340-40-25-5 | Capitalize fulfillment costs only when they relate to a specific contract, create resources used to perform, and are expected to be recovered. | RH |
| Contract costs: amortization | 340-40-35-1 | Amortize consistently with the transfer of the related goods or services, including specific anticipated contracts such as renewals. | RH |

## Not codified here, or open

- No general onerous-contract model exists in ASC 606. Loss provisions for contracts in
  the scope of ASC 605-35 (construction and production type) remain there. For other
  services, name the loss and route it to the preparer. The skill does not measure it.
- TRG discussions on termination clauses are nonauthoritative. Cite them as "TRG
  discussion", not by memo number.
